How do I paginate a Django queryset?

I’m building a question feed and want to keep the page links readable. How should I handle a missing or invalid page number?

4 answers Oct 25, 2026
django python

4 answers

8

Use Django’s Paginator to split your queryset into pages. Catch PageNotAnInteger and EmptyPage, then return the first page when the value is invalid.
